Dubai is engineered for the view-bar. Half the hotels have a rooftop or 40th-floor bar built around a Burj Khalifa shot. Here are the ones where the drinks are actually worth it, picked by view angle, dress code, and whether the music is loud enough to ruin a conversation.

## At.mosphere — Burj Khalifa floor 122

The highest bar in the world until SKY View opened. 442 metres up. Dress code: jacket. Classic cocktails 100–180 AED. The one time it's actually fine to pay 180 AED for a Negroni.

## Vault — JW Marriott Marquis floor 71

71st floor rooftop with Marina and Downtown both visible. Lower-key than Address Sky View, better service, cocktails 90–130 AED.

## Treehouse — Taj Dubai

Rooftop bar with Burj Khalifa head-on from Business Bay. Garden-themed with foliage walls. Signature cocktails 90–120 AED, best at 18:30 for the fountain show.

## Pierchic Bar — Al Qasr

Over-water bar with Burj Al Arab in frame. Smaller than the restaurant, same iconic view. Smart-casual, cocktails 80–130 AED.

## Attiko Bar — W The Palm

Palm Jumeirah rooftop with Marina skyline. Younger crowd, DJs Thursday-Saturday. Cocktails 85–130 AED.
