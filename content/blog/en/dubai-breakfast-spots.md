Dubai's breakfast scene has moved beyond hotel buffets. Specialty coffee shops, Lebanese flatbread bakeries, and Pakistani chaiwala spots open at 06:30. Here are the ones worth setting an alarm for.

## Common Grounds — Al Barsha, Business Bay, more

Local specialty coffee chain. Flat whites, well-made eggs on sourdough, acai bowls. 50–90 AED for breakfast + coffee. Several locations across the city, all solid.

## Logma — Boxpark, City Walk

Modern Emirati breakfast: khameer (local flatbread) with cheese and honey, chebab (local pancakes), karak chai. 40–70 AED per plate. Compact, Instagram-friendly.

## Zuma breakfast — DIFC

Japanese-leaning breakfast at the Michelin-starred Zuma Dubai. Not cheap (130–220 AED per plate) but one of the most refined breakfasts in town.

## Project Chaiwala — Satwa, City Walk, Alserkal

Pakistani chai specialists — cardamom karak, masala, saffron, with filled parathas and anda bhurji (spiced eggs). 25–55 AED. Opens 07:00.

## Hotel buffets

The classic: Palace Downtown's Thiptara buffet (320 AED, Burj view), Four Seasons Jumeirah's SUQ (280 AED, beachfront), Atlantis Saffron (380 AED, loudest but biggest selection).
