Gold in Dubai does not have one price. There's a spot rate that changes every morning, a making charge that varies per shop, and 5% VAT at the till. Add tax-free refunds for tourists and the picture gets layered. Here's exactly how a gram of 22K is priced from the moment you walk into a shop to the moment you pay.

## The daily spot rate

The Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group publishes the morning spot rate per gram in all four karats — 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K — and shops update their window displays to match. The rate tracks the London fixing, converted to AED, so a strong dollar day pushes prices up in dirhams even if dollar gold stayed flat. You can see today's number on jewellery-group displays and on apps like Gold Rate Dubai.

## Making charge

On top of the metal cost sits labour. A plain gold bangle might carry 10–15 AED per gram in making; a diamond-set wedding set can run 30–60 AED per gram. The making is where you negotiate — it's up to the shop and often inflated on sticker so there's room to come down. Ask for the making charge separately on your bill; it's your lever.

## VAT and tax-free

The UAE charges 5% VAT on finished gold jewellery (but not on investment-grade bars of 99%+ purity). Tourists can reclaim most of the VAT through the Planet tax-free scheme: ask the shop to issue a tax-free receipt, validate at the airport Planet kiosk before check-in, and the refund hits your card or comes out as cash minus a processing fee.

## Is Dubai actually cheaper?

On the gold metal itself, Dubai is competitive but not dramatically cheaper than Europe or the US — the global market sets the floor. Where Dubai wins is the making charge: in London or New York a crafted piece might carry 100%+ markup over melt value, while Dubai shops routinely sell closer to melt plus 15–25%. The practical answer: Dubai is cheaper on crafted pieces, roughly even on plain investment bars.
