Jumeirah is Dubai's old coastal strip, running from the Palm back toward the city, and most of the beachfront restaurants here have the Burj Al Arab in frame. Here's the shortlist — from fine dining with jackets to beach shacks with your feet in the sand.

## Pierchic — Al Qasr, Madinat Jumeirah

Over-water Mediterranean restaurant on a pier reaching into the Gulf. The classic Burj Al Arab photo view. Mains 150–280 AED. Smart dress, reservations essential, sunset slots book 2 weeks ahead.

## Nathan Outlaw — Burj Al Arab

The British chef's seafood tasting menu inside the Burj Al Arab's Al Mahara (underwater aquarium room). 850–1,200 AED for the full experience. One of Dubai's most memorable sits.

## The Beach House — Four Seasons Jumeirah

Mediterranean seafood on sand with Burj Al Arab in view. Beach-casual (resort wear). Mains 180–320 AED. Lunch is quieter and cheaper than dinner.

## Shimmers — Mina A' Salam

Greek-Mediterranean with the Burj Al Arab directly behind. Casual by Dubai standards — flip-flops OK at lunch. Mains 110–220 AED. One of the best golden-hour locations in the city.

## Nikki Beach Restaurant — Pearl Jumeirah

The classic Nikki beach-club experience adapted to Dubai: beach, loungers, Mediterranean food, DJ Saturdays. Day-pass with F&B credit 400–600 AED. Fun, not quiet.
