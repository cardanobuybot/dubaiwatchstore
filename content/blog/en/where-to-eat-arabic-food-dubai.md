Dubai's international food scene gets the magazine covers, but the city also hides serious Arabic food — Emirati camel and machboos, Lebanese mezze from third-generation families, Yemeni mandi tossed in ash. Most of it is in Deira or Al Satwa, not Downtown.

## Al Fanar — Festival City and other locations

The headline Emirati restaurant — pearl-diving-themed interior, authentic machboos, luqaimat (date dumplings), and camel burger if you're brave. Mains 60–140 AED. Family-friendly.

## Al Nafoorah — Jumeirah Zabeel Saray

Lebanese grill at hotel standards: 30+ cold mezze, perfect kibbeh, charcoal-grilled lamb. Mains 100–220 AED. The reference for high-end Lebanese in the city.

## Bu Qtair — Jumeirah Beach Road

The classic shack. No reservations, no menu — just whole grilled fish with Pakistani masala rice. Dinner for two around 100 AED. Expect a queue; worth every minute.

## Mandi House — Deira

Yemeni mandi — chicken or lamb slow-cooked over wood ash and served on a mountain of basmati. Mains 35–80 AED. Casual, no alcohol, eat with your right hand.

## Ravi Restaurant — Al Satwa

A Dubai institution — technically Pakistani but with full Arabic menu overlap. Legendary chicken karahi, perfect naan. Dinner for two 60–100 AED. Open 24 hours.
