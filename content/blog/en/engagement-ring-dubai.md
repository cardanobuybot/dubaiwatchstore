Dubai is one of the better places in the world to buy an engagement ring — strong wholesale infrastructure, honest certification, and specialist workshops that will build to your spec in a week. Here's how to approach it from the budget conversation to the fitting.

## Budget benchmarks

Entry solitaire with 0.3ct GIA stone in 18K setting: 6,000–9,000 AED. Classic 0.7ct GIA G-VS1 solitaire: 18,000–28,000 AED. 1ct D-VVS2 ideal-cut: 50,000–80,000 AED. Lab-grown stones at the same specs run 50–70% less. These assume Dubai Diamond Park or Gold & Diamond Park pricing, not Cartier or Tiffany.

## Where to buy

Three sensible paths: (1) a branded boutique in Dubai Mall if you want a certified brand name (Tiffany, Graff, Harry Winston) — fixed prices, international service; (2) Gold & Diamond Park for custom work with a local jeweller — 20–30% cheaper, 7–14 days turnaround; (3) Dubai Diamond Exchange for the stone, with a separate workshop for the setting — cheapest for large stones but needs appointments.

## Diamond or setting first?

Buy the stone first, settle the setting after. The stone is the biggest line item and the setting adapts around it. Specify the 4Cs — most couples optimise for cut (biggest visible impact) then colour, then clarity, with carat as the budget lever.

## What to confirm before you pay

Certificate (GIA preferred above 0.7ct), laser serial matching the girdle inscription, metal karat (18K is standard for daily-wear rings), ring size (resize guarantee), warranty on the setting, and a tax-free receipt for the airport.
