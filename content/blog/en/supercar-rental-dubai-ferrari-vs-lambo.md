Both Italian supercar brands rent out across Dubai, both start at around 2,200 AED/day, both deliver 90% of the same experience to a tourist for a few hours of driving. The honest difference is model fit and brand feel. Here's how to decide.

## On price

Entry-level Ferrari (488 GTB, Portofino) and entry-level Lamborghini (Huracan, Urus) rent for roughly the same: 2,200–3,500 AED/day. Top of fleet — SF90 vs Aventador — Ferrari is slightly cheaper per day. Weekly rates narrow the gap further.

## On driving feel

Ferrari is more precise, quieter, more sophisticated — easier to drive hard. Lamborghini is louder, more theatrical, more work. For twisty roads (Jebel Hafeet mountain) Ferrari wins. For sheer presence on Marina Walk, Lamborghini wins.

## On brand & photos

Instagram-wise, Lamborghini has stronger visual impact — scissor doors, aggressive angles, bright colours. Ferrari is more classic red/yellow and understated. Both get stares in Dubai; the Lambo gets more.

## On rental logistics

Both deliver to hotels. Deposits are similar. The main real difference: Ferrari insurance deductibles tend to be slightly lower, Lamborghini companies sometimes offer tour packages (Burj Khalifa + supercar) that Ferrari firms don't.

## The quick answer

First supercar rental, want to drive — Ferrari 488 or Portofino. First rental, want photos and sound — Lamborghini Huracan. Family trip — Urus. Statement day — Aventador or SF90.
