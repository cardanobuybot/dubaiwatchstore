Dubai doesn't do subtle. The city's rooftop restaurants are glass-walled, pool-side, often 50 floors up and almost always lit for Instagram. Here are the ones that deserve the reservation — picked by the view, the food, and whether the vibe matches the price.

## CÉ LA VI — Address Sky View, Downtown

Modern Asian on floor 54 with the Burj Khalifa framed in a window. Infinity pool at sunset. Dress smart-casual. Main courses 180–350 AED. Reservations essential; weekend brunch is a Dubai institution.

## SUSHISAMBA — Palm Tower, Palm Jumeirah

Japanese-Peruvian-Brazilian on floor 51 of the Palm Tower. Floor-to-ceiling view of Palm, Marina and the Gulf. Robata and ceviche signatures. Mains 150–300 AED. Great for drinks even if you don't eat.

## ATTIKO — W Dubai, The Palm

Pan-Asian with a huge outdoor terrace facing Marina. DJ sets Thu–Sat. Lively, 25–35 crowd. Mains 160–280 AED. Smart-casual.

## Dinner in the Sky

Not a restaurant but worth mentioning: 22 diners strapped into seats hoisted 50m into the air by a crane for 90 minutes of tasting menu. 650–895 AED per person. Memorable, pricey, weather-dependent.

## iris Dubai — Meydan

Rooftop garden bar/restaurant away from the main tourist belt. Mediterranean-Asian fusion. 180–280 AED mains. Great sunset angle, less corporate feel than Downtown.
