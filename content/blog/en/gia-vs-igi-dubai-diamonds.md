A diamond without a certificate is just a shiny stone. In Dubai three labs dominate: GIA (American, strictest), IGI (Belgian/Indian, widely used), and HRD (Belgian, strong in Europe). The certificate affects price by 10–20% for the same stone — here's how to tell them apart and when each makes sense.

## GIA — the gold standard

The Gemological Institute of America is the oldest and strictest grading lab. A GIA cert carries a price premium of 10–20% on the same stone because grading is conservative — a GIA G-VS2 might grade F-VS1 at IGI. For investment-grade stones above 1 carat, GIA is the safest paperwork.

## IGI — the Dubai workhorse

The International Gemological Institute has large labs in Antwerp, Mumbai and Dubai. Faster turnaround, slightly more generous grading, lower certification fees. For stones under 1 carat and most lab-grown diamonds, IGI is standard in Dubai and completely trustworthy.

## HRD — European preference

Hoge Raad voor Diamant, the Antwerp council, is the main European alternative. Rare in Dubai mass-market but shows up at wholesale level and specialist designers. Grading is similar to GIA, slightly less strict.

## What to check on the certificate

Match the laser-engraved number on the girdle (visible under 10x loupe) to the certificate serial. Verify the certificate on the lab's website — GIA.edu, IGI.org, HRDAntwerp.com all have report lookup. Confirm the stone's measurements (length × width × depth in mm) match what the jeweller shows you.
