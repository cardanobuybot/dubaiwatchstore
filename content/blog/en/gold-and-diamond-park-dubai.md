The Gold & Diamond Park is Dubai's specialist jewellery mall — not a tourist attraction but a working marketplace where locals buy wedding sets and expats commission bespoke rings. It's a 10-minute drive from Mall of the Emirates and worth the detour if you want in-house workshops, honest making charges and no mall crowds.

## The building

A single-floor complex on Sheikh Zayed Road with over 100 shops arranged around a central corridor. Air-conditioned, free parking, open 10:00–22:00 daily (Fridays from 14:00). Metro: Noor Bank station, 10-minute walk.

## What's inside

Mostly specialist jewellers — some branded (Damas, Joyalukkas branch), many independent. On-site workshops mean you can commission a custom design, see progress, and pick up in 3–7 days. There's a Planet tax-free counter inside the mall itself for faster refunds.

## How prices compare

Making charges typically 15–20% lower than Dubai Mall, 5–10% higher than Deira Souk. Diamonds often beat the souk because the park concentrates specialist stone dealers and certification is standard. For custom work, this is the cheapest serious option in the city.

## Who it's for

Engagement rings, wedding sets, significant investment pieces, custom work. Not the best place for a quick "souvenir chain" — the souk is faster and friendlier. Dress business-casual; the vibe is quieter than the souk.
