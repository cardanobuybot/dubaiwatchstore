Dubai allows alcohol sales but with rules. Tourists can buy from duty-free at the airport, from two licensed off-licence chains (MMI and African+Eastern), and consume at any licensed restaurant or bar — which means most hotel-attached venues. Here's exactly how it works.

## Dubai Airport duty-free

On arrival, the Dubai Duty Free alcohol shop is right after passport control at every terminal. Allowance: 4 litres per adult non-Muslim visitor. Prices are competitive with European duty-free. Buy here if you plan to drink in your hotel room — hotels charge 300–500% markup.

## MMI and African+Eastern — off-licence shops

The two licensed retailers for off-premise alcohol in Dubai. Over 20 shops across the city. Open 10:00–22:00 Mon–Sat, closed Fridays. Tourists need to show passport (not a tourist alcohol licence — those have been scrapped). 30% tourist/VAT discount at the till.

## Hotel bars and restaurants

Any venue inside a hotel (or technically licensed as a hotel outlet) can serve alcohol. This covers most upscale restaurants, nightclubs, lounges and rooftop bars. Standalone restaurants rarely hold licences — assume "no alcohol" unless you see a wine list.

## Public drinking rules

Drinking in public — streets, beaches, parks — is illegal. Being drunk in public is prosecutable. Hotel beaches and licensed beach clubs are fine. Taxi home from any bar; no DUI tolerance.
