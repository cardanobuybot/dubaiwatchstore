A desert safari is the one Dubai experience that lets you escape the glass towers. 45 minutes out of Downtown you're in the red Lahbab or Al Marmoom dunes, in a 4x4 pitching through sand at 60 km/h. Here's what each safari type delivers and what the price actually buys you.

## Evening safari — the classic

Pickup 15:00, 45-minute drive, 30 minutes of dune bashing, sandboarding and camel ride, sunset photos, dinner at a Bedouin camp (buffet, henna, belly dance, tanoura spinner, shisha), drop-off by 22:00. Average price: 150–250 AED per adult. Best for first-timers.

## Morning safari — the active option

Pickup 07:00, dune bashing and sandboarding in cooler weather, camel riding, back by 12:00. No dinner. Price: 100–150 AED. Choose this if you want the thrill without the night entertainment.

## Overnight safari — the real desert

Pickup 15:00, dune bashing + full camp night under stars, breakfast, back by 09:00 next day. Price: 400–600 AED. Best for photographers (Milky Way visible in winter), couples, anyone who wants silence after the shows end.

## What to wear and bring

Light long sleeves and trousers (sand gets everywhere, nights are cold Nov–Mar), closed shoes for sand-boarding, sunglasses. The camp is modestly dressed — no bikinis. Alcohol is sold at the camp bar if the operator holds a licence.
