Dubai is the easiest city in the world to rent a Ferrari — rental companies operate 24/7, you need only a valid licence and a credit card, and the roads are built for it (smooth, straight, policed by speed cameras rather than cops). Here's the full process from booking to delivery.

## Daily prices by model

488 GTB: 2,200–2,800 AED/day. Portofino: 2,500–3,000 AED. F8 Tributo: 3,000–3,800 AED. SF90 Stradale: 4,500–6,000 AED. Roma: 2,800–3,400 AED. Weekly rentals are usually 15–20% cheaper per day. Add 500 AED/day for a chauffeur if you prefer to be driven.

## What's included

Standard package: 100–250 km/day free (overage AED 5–10/km), basic insurance with a hefty deductible (10,000–20,000 AED), 24/7 roadside assistance, free hotel delivery across Dubai. Not included: fuel (return it full) and Salik toll charges (billed after).

## Deposit and papers

Deposit via credit card hold: 5,000–15,000 AED depending on model. Required: valid driving licence (home-country licence works for 30 days for tourists; UAE/GCC licences accepted outright; some companies also accept IDP), passport copy, matching credit card. Minimum age usually 25.

## Best companies

Established: Milele Rent a Car, Be Vip Rent a Car, Fast Rent a Car, Rotana Star, One Click Drive (aggregator). Avoid walk-ins at Marina — compare on booking.com or Carswitch 48 hours before you need the car.

## Where to drive

Sheikh Zayed Road (120 km/h limit, speed cameras everywhere), Jebel Hafeet mountain road (near Al Ain, 1,200-metre climb with hairpins — the only real driving road near Dubai), and the coastal road along Palm Jumeirah for photos.
