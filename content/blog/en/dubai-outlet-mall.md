Dubai has two outlet shopping destinations — The Outlet Village for luxury and Dubai Outlet Mall for mid-range. Discounts run 30–75% off original retail year-round and reach 90% during the Dubai Shopping Festival (January) and the Dubai Summer Surprises (July–August).

## The Outlet Village — luxury discounts

Open-air Mediterranean-style village on the Jebel Ali side of town. Brands: Armani, Boss, Burberry, Coach, Diesel, Michael Kors, Nike Factory, Prada, Tommy Hilfiger, Versace. Average 40–60% off previous-season stock. Food court and valet parking. A 25-minute drive from Dubai Marina.

## Dubai Outlet Mall — mid-range workhorse

On Dubai–Al Ain Road, 20 minutes past The Outlet Village. 240 stores covering mid-range international and Middle East labels: Adidas, Levi's, Guess, Charles & Keith, Mango, Puma, Steve Madden, Timberland. Discounts 30–75% year-round. Indoor, air-conditioned, with a food court and a bowling alley.

## When to go

Weekday mornings are quietest. For the deepest discounts, aim for mid-January (Dubai Shopping Festival clearance) or late July–August (Dubai Summer Surprises). Avoid Friday afternoons.

## VAT and tax-free

Same Planet tax-free scheme as the city malls. Both outlets have in-house Planet counters for faster paperwork. 5% VAT refundable on items over 250 AED minus the processing fee.
