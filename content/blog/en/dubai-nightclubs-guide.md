Dubai nightlife is hotel-anchored, dress-coded, and expensive. There's no cover charge most places, but drinks are 50–120 AED, tables start at 1,500 AED, and the queue is long if you're not on a guestlist. Here are the main spots.

## Soho Garden — Meydan

The biggest nightlife complex in Dubai: four venues under one roof (nightclub, lounge, Japanese-themed outdoor, rooftop). Big-name DJs weekly (Tiesto, Diplo, FISHER tier). Expect 150+ AED cover for signature nights, tables from 2,500 AED.

## White Dubai — Meydan Grandstand

Rooftop open-air club with Downtown view. Hip-hop and commercial dance focused. Dubai's biggest launch spot — AlbuMarco Carola, David Guetta have played here. 500–1,500 AED tables, cover 150 AED.

## Base Dubai — Dubai Design District

Underground club with international techno/house residencies: Honey Dijon, Dixon, Jamie xx, Marco Carola. Grittier dress code, serious music crowd. Cover 100–250 AED, tables 2,000+ AED.

## Zero Gravity — Dubai Marina

Beach club by day, nightclub by Friday night. Pool parties, international headliners, DJ sets poolside. Day pass + evening event combo 300–500 AED.

## Secret Room — Address Hotel Downtown

Smaller, more polished lounge — hip-hop/R&B, Dubai bottle-service crowd. Downtown location, Burj Khalifa view. Tables from 1,800 AED, cover 100–200 AED.
