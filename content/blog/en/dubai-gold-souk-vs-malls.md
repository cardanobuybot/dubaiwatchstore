You can buy gold in Dubai in four very different places: the old Deira Gold Souk, inside Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates, at the specialist Gold & Diamond Park on Sheikh Zayed Road, and online with doorstep delivery. Each one has a different crowd, a different making charge, and a different browsing experience. Here's how to pick.

## Deira Gold Souk — best for grammage

The historic covered street has 300+ shops, mostly family-run, with low overheads and low making charges. Great for plain gold chains, bangles and investment pieces where you want to pay close to melt value. Downside: narrow aisles, no air-con in the alleys, harder negotiation if you don't like haggling.

## Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates — best for brand jewellery

Fixed prices, air-con, no haggling but no surprises either. Damas, Joyalukkas, Tiffany, Cartier, Bulgari and Van Cleef all have mall boutiques. Making charges are higher than the souk by 20–40%, but you get international warranties and the ability to compare designs calmly.

## Gold & Diamond Park — best for custom work

A specialist mall off Sheikh Zayed Road near Mall of the Emirates with 100+ jewellers and in-house workshops. Prices sit between souk and mainstream mall. This is where you commission a custom engagement ring, a copy of a design you saw on Instagram, or a bespoke bangle — turnaround is often 3–7 days.

## Online — best for daily deliveries

Platforms like Malabar, Joyalukkas and Damas now ship nationwide with same-day delivery in Dubai. Prices lock when you order (useful on volatile gold days) and the pieces come from the same stock as the stores.
