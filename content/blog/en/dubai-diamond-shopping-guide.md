Dubai has become one of the biggest diamond markets in the world — the Dubai Diamond Exchange processes billions in rough and polished stones every year. For a tourist that means strong wholesale infrastructure, honest certification, and prices 20–40% below London or New York retail. Here's how to buy without being overwhelmed.

## Where to buy

Three main places: the Gold & Diamond Park off Sheikh Zayed Road (specialist workshops, custom work), Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates (international brands — Tiffany, Graff, Cartier), and the Dubai Diamond Exchange inside Almas Tower (wholesale, by appointment, best for 1ct+ stones).

## The 4Cs in practice

Carat (size), colour (D is colourless, Z is yellow), clarity (FL to I3), cut (how well it reflects light). In Dubai, D-F VVS1-VS2 excellent-cut stones carry a premium of 20–30% over G-H SI1-SI2. For eye-clean budget pieces, G colour and SI1 clarity give you most of the sparkle at 70% of the price.

## Certification

Insist on a GIA, IGI or HRD certificate for anything above 0.3 carats. GIA is the most conservative (and most expensive to issue); IGI is popular in Dubai and widely trusted. The certificate is laser-engraved into the stone's girdle so you can verify under 10x magnification.

## Lab-grown vs natural

Dubai sells both. Lab-grown diamonds are chemically identical and cost 60–80% less. Mall boutiques are starting to carry lab-grown lines (Pandora, local designers); specialist jewellers at Gold & Diamond Park will quote both side-by-side.

## Haggling and extras

On branded pieces (Tiffany, Cartier) prices are fixed. On non-branded or custom work, 10–20% off the asking is reasonable. Always get the certificate included and ask for free resizing or minor adjustments within 30 days.
