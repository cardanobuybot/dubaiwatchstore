Three Lamborghinis dominate Dubai's rental fleet: the Huracan (classic V10 supercar), the Urus (super-SUV, surprisingly practical), and the Aventador (V12, loudest, hardest to drive). Each costs a different amount and gives a different experience. Here's the real-world picture.

## Huracan — the Instagram pick

4,200 cm³ V10, 610 hp, 0–100 in 3.2s. Daily rate: 2,500–3,500 AED. Low-slung, loud, surprisingly civilised in traffic. Great for Marina photos and Sheikh Zayed Road cruising. Harder on speed-bumps than you'd expect — Dubai parking lot ramps need care.

## Urus — the practical super-SUV

650 hp V8 twin-turbo, 0–100 in 3.6s but 850 Nm torque that moves the family. Daily rate: 2,500–3,200 AED. Fits four adults with luggage, works on bumpy roads, and is more usable in day-to-day Dubai than any coupe. The surprise-pick if you have kids or a long trip to Al Ain.

## Aventador — the big-boy

V12, 740 hp, 0–100 in 2.9s. Daily rate: 5,000–7,000 AED. Loudest supercar in the fleet. Scissor doors, carbon everywhere, hard to park, and the deductible on an accident is terrifying. Rent for a photo day, not a week.

## Deposits and licence

Deposit on all three: 10,000–25,000 AED. Minimum age: 25 (Aventador often 30+). Licence: home country for 30 days, GCC/UAE always valid, IDP optional but useful. Comprehensive insurance strongly recommended on the Aventador — the car's base value exceeds 1.5 million AED.
