Brunch in Dubai is not toast and coffee. It's 4 hours of free-flowing food and drinks, usually noon to 16:00, usually in a hotel, usually priced 300–900 AED per adult. It's the city's definitive weekend tradition. Here are the serious ones.

## Bubbalicious — Westin Mina Seyahi

The big-ticket brunch — 10 food stations, free-flowing champagne, beach access included. 795–895 AED with bubbly, 495 soft drinks. Loud, family-friendly, legendary.


## Perry & Blackwelder's — Souk Madinat Jumeirah

Smokehouse-style brunch with BBQ, live blues band and ribs. 275 AED soft, 395 AED house drinks. Smaller scale, better for groups of 4–6.

## Nobu Brunch — Atlantis The Palm

Japanese-Peruvian menu brunch, sushi stations and sake flights. 650 AED, 795 with alcohol. Sophisticated, less chaotic than Westin.

## Yum! Cha — Taj Dubai

Chinese weekend brunch — dim sum, Peking duck, DJ, Downtown views. 395 AED soft, 495 AED house drinks. Good value for the quality.

## Weezbrunch — Four Seasons DIFC

Modern European brunch, DIFC crowd (bankers), high-end. 650 AED, 850 with Veuve. Dress code: elegant.
