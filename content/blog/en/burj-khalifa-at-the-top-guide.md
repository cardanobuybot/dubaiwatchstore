The Burj Khalifa has four observation decks on floors 124, 125, 148 and 152. Each offers a different price, a different view and a different crowd. Here's how to pick, when to go, and how to time your visit around the Dubai Fountain show below.

## At The Top (124–125)

The entry-level deck. Combined indoor and outdoor terrace on floors 124 and 125 at 452 metres. Standard adult: 169 AED on weekdays, 220 AED weekends. Views span the Downtown skyline, Business Bay, and the coast. Open 09:00–23:00.

## At The Top SKY (148)

The premium deck at 555 metres — second-highest observation platform in the world. Includes a boarding pass experience with refreshments. Standard adult: 395 AED, prime time 475 AED. Books out often — reserve 48 hours ahead.

## The Lounge (152–154)

The top-floor experience: all-you-can-eat snacks and drinks on floors 152, 153, 154. Smaller crowds, better service. Adult: 629 AED prime time. Comes with the "world's highest lounge" bragging rights.

## When to go

Sunset is the money shot — book a slot that lets you be on deck 30 minutes before sunset so you see day, dusk and lit-city sequentially. The Dubai Fountain plays below every 30 minutes from 18:00; your deck view synchronises with music piped into At The Top.
