The Palm Jumeirah is Dubai's man-made archipelago shaped like a palm tree, 7 kilometres of beach and 25,000 residents. A full day covers two beaches, one top-floor viewpoint, lunch overlooking the Atlantis, and a monorail ride down the trunk. Here's the day done right.

## Morning: The View at The Palm

Start at the Palm Tower (240 metres), floor 52, for the 360° observation deck. Entry 100 AED, 30-minute visit, best in morning when light is soft. Book online — walk-ups queue.

## Late morning: Nakheel Mall + monorail

Nakheel Mall sits under the Palm Tower — Boots, Carrefour, 60+ restaurants. From the mall's monorail station, take the single-line monorail (25 AED one-way) down the Palm's trunk to Atlantis — 10 minutes with views both sides.

## Lunch: pick one

Nobu at Atlantis (upscale Japanese, book ahead), Pierchic (over-water Mediterranean, iconic photo), White Beach Club (Mediterranean with pool access, pricey but makes the day). Budget option: 101 Lounge & Bar at One&Only Royal Mirage with a boat ride from Atlantis dock.

## Afternoon: Atlantis or beach

Two choices: Aquaventure Waterpark at Atlantis (biggest in the Middle East, from 299 AED) or a public beach club with day passes (Zero Gravity next to Dubai Marina — 100 AED weekdays, 200 AED weekends, pool and sand).

## Evening

Return to the Palm Tower rooftop (Aura Skypool — world's highest 360° infinity pool) for sunset drinks. Or finish at Nobu or Hakkasan on the Atlantis side.
